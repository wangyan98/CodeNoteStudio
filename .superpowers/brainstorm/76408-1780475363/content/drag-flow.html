Drag-and-Drop Interaction Flow

Mind Map: drag node to reorder or reparent

Case 1: Reorder (drag between siblings)

Drag "Node B" below "Node C" — siblings shift to make room

Node C

Case 2: Reparent (drag onto another node)

Drag "Node B" onto "Node C" — target gets yellow border, drop makes B a child of C

After drop
